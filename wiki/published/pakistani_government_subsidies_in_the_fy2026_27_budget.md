# Summary

Overview of the subsidies allocated in Pakistan's federal budget for FY2026-27, including green component breakdowns across different sectors.

# Main Content

In the federal budget for FY2026-27, total current expenditures for subsidies are budgeted at Rs. 1,091,000 million (pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27, lines 346-441). This represents a budget allocation rather than actual payments. Within the subsidies framework, Table 19 outlines the green component in subsidies for FY2026-27, totaling Rs. 476,000 million across multiple sectors (pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27, lines 968-1081). The sectoral breakdown of this green component includes:
- Energy: Mitigation classification under Category A (Directly Favorable) with Rs. 423,000 million (pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27, lines 968-1081).
- Food: Adaptation classification under Category B (Indirectly Favorable) with Rs. 19,000 million (pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27, lines 968-1081).
- Industries: Mitigation classification under Category A with Rs. 8,000 million (pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27, lines 968-1081).
- Transport: Mitigation classification under Category A with Rs. 5,000 million (pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27, lines 968-1081).
- Agriculture: Adaptation classification under Category B with Rs. 21,000 million (pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27, lines 968-1081).

# Key Facts

- The FY2026-27 budget allocation for subsidies is Rs. 1,091,000 million.
- The total green component in subsidies for FY2026-27 is budgeted at Rs. 476,000 million.
- Energy sector green subsidies amount to Rs. 423,000 million (Mitigation, Category A).
- Agricultural green subsidies amount to Rs. 21,000 million (Adaptation, Category B).
- Food sector green subsidies amount to Rs. 19,000 million (Adaptation, Category B).
- Industries sector green subsidies amount to Rs. 8,000 million (Mitigation, Category A).
- Transport sector green subsidies amount to Rs. 5,000 million (Mitigation, Category A).
