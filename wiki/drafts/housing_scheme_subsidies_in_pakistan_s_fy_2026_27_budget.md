# Summary

Overview of the budget allocations for housing schemes and related housing sector subsidies in Pakistan for the financial year 2026-27 based on federal budget estimates.

# Main Content

In Pakistan's FY 2026-27 budget, specific housing-related subsidies are allocated under Table 12 (Subsidies). The individual allocations for housing schemes and programs for the financial year 2026-27 are as follows: Mera Pakistan Mera Ghar Scheme (Mark-up Subsidy on Housing Finance Scheme) is allocated Rs. 5,000 million (pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27, lines 612-697), and the PM Apna Ghar Program is allocated Rs. 71,000 million (pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27, lines 688-780). Other line items related to housing, such as the Subsidy to Naya Pakistan Housing Authority, Mark-up subsidy for Low Cost Housing, Housing Sector Subsidy, and Mark-up subsidy and Risk Sharing Scheme for Affordable Housing Finance, are listed with no amount (-), a dash indicating no amount listed rather than zero (pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27, lines 612-697, 688-780). 

To find the combined total for housing schemes with active allocations in FY 2026-27, we add the individual active amounts: Rs. 5,000 million (Mera Pakistan Mera Ghar Scheme) plus Rs. 71,000 million (PM Apna Ghar Program), resulting in a combined total of Rs. 76,000 million (pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27, lines 612-697, 688-780). These figures represent budget allocations rather than actual payments (pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27, lines 1-88).

# Key Facts

- The FY 2026-27 budget allocation for the Mera Pakistan Mera Ghar Scheme (Mark-up Subsidy on Housing Finance Scheme) is Rs. 5,000 million (pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27, lines 612-697).
- The FY 2026-27 budget allocation for the PM Apna Ghar Program is Rs. 71,000 million (pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27, lines 688-780).
- The combined total of the active housing scheme budget allocations for FY 2026-27 is Rs. 76,000 million, calculated by adding Rs. 5,000 million and Rs. 71,000 million (pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27, lines 612-697, 688-780).
- Other housing-related entries such as Naya Pakistan Housing Authority, Mark-up subsidy for Low Cost Housing, Housing Sector Subsidy, and Mark-up Subsidy and Risk Sharing Scheme for Affordable Housing Finance have no amounts listed (-) for FY 2026-27 (pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27, lines 612-697, 688-780).
