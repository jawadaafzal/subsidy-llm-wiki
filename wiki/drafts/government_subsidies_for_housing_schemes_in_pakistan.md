# Summary

Overview of government budget allocations and figures related to housing scheme subsidies in Pakistan based on federal budget estimates.

# Main Content

Government subsidy allocations for housing schemes are detailed in the federal budget documents. The available evidence outlines specific budget allocations and revised figures for various housing initiatives, distinguishing between budgeted amounts and other figures. For instance, the subsidy to the Naya Pakistan Housing Authority was allocated at Rs. 1,000 million in the 2025-26 budget, with no figures listed for the revised 2025-26 or 2026-27 budget [pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27, lines 650-652]. The Mera Pakistan Mera Ghar Scheme (Mark-up Subsidy on Housing Finance Scheme) shows Rs. 5,000 million allocated in the 2026-27 budget, with no allocations listed for budget or revised 2025-26 [pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27, lines 653-655]. Additionally, the Mark-up subsidy for Low Cost Housing had a budget allocation of Rs. 5,000 million and a revised allocation of Rs. 5,000 million for 2025-26, but no allocation for 2026-27 [pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27, lines 671-673]. Housing Sector Subsidy was budgeted at Rs. 5,000 million for 2025-26, with no amounts for the revised 2025-26 or 2026-27 periods [pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27, lines 676-678]. Furthermore, the Markup Subsidy and Risk Sharing Scheme for Affordable Housing Finance recorded a revised figure of Rs. 5,000 million for 2025-26, with zero or unlisted amounts for the other periods [pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27, lines 686-688], and the PM Apna Ghar Program recorded Rs. 71,000 million in the 2026-27 budget [pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27, lines 689-691].

# Key Facts

- Subsidy to Naya Pakistan Housing Authority had a 2025-26 budget allocation of Rs. 1,000 million [pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27, lines 650-652].
- Mera Pakistan Mera Ghar Scheme (Mark-up Subsidy on Housing Finance Scheme) was allocated Rs. 5,000 million in the 2026-27 budget [pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27, lines 653-655].
- Mark-up subsidy for Low Cost Housing was allocated Rs. 5,000 million in both the 2025-26 budget and the 2025-26 revised estimates [pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27, lines 671-673].
- Housing Sector Subsidy had a 2025-26 budget allocation of Rs. 5,000 million [pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27, lines 676-678].
- Markup Subsidy and Risk Sharing Scheme for Affordable Housing Finance had a revised 2025-26 amount of Rs. 5,000 million [pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27, lines 686-688].
- PM Apna Ghar Program was allocated Rs. 71,000 million in the 2026-27 budget [pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27, lines 689-691].
