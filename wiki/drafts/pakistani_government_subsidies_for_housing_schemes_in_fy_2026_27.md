# Summary

An overview of housing scheme subsidy allocations in Pakistan's federal budget for the financial year 2026-27 based on official source data.

# Main Content

For the financial year (FY) 2026-27 budget, the verified combined total allocated for housing sector subsidies is Rs. 76,000 million. This combined total is derived from the specific budget allocations listed in the Table 12 Subsidies for the requested financial year column (Budget 2026-27). The individual scheme amounts for FY 2026-27 include: Mera Pakistan Mera Ghar Scheme (Mark-up Subsidy on Housing Finance Scheme) with Rs. 5,000 million (source line 637), and PM Apna Ghar Program with Rs. 71,000 million (source line 651). Other housing-related lines such as the Subsidy to Naya Pakistan Housing Authority, Mark-up subsidy for Low Cost Housing, Housing Sector Subsidy, and Markup Subsidy and Risk Sharing Scheme for Affordable Housing Finance have no amounts listed (indicated by a dash) for FY 2026-27 (source lines 636, 644, 646, 650). These figures represent budgeted allocations rather than actual payments.

# Key Facts

- The combined total subsidy allocated to housing schemes in the FY 2026-27 budget is Rs. 76,000 million.
- Mera Pakistan Mera Ghar Scheme (Mark-up Subsidy on Housing Finance Scheme) is allocated Rs. 5,000 million in the FY 2026-27 budget.
- PM Apna Ghar Program is allocated Rs. 71,000 million in the FY 2026-27 budget.
- Subsidy to Naya Pakistan Housing Authority has no amount listed for FY 2026-27.
- Mark-up subsidy for Low Cost Housing, Housing Sector Subsidy, and Markup Subsidy and Risk Sharing Scheme for Affordable Housing Finance have no amounts listed for FY 2026-27.
