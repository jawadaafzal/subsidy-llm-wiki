\# Summary



Official National Electricity Plan 2023-27 published by the Government of Pakistan. It describes policy measures for rationalizing electricity subsidies, targeting eligible residential and agricultural consumers, and managing cross-subsidies.



\# Main Content



\- Publisher: Government of Pakistan, Ministry of Energy (Power Division).

\- Original PDF: raw/national\_electricity\_plan\_2023\_27.pdf

\- Extracted Markdown: raw/national\_electricity\_plan\_2023\_27.md

\- Priority Area 14 covers Subsidy Rationalization.

\- Strategic Directive 82 addresses subsidies for protected residential consumers.

\- Strategic Directive 83 addresses subsidies for eligible agricultural consumers.

\- Strategic Directive 84 addresses cross-subsidies.

\- Strategic Directive 85 addresses reappropriation of annual allocated subsidies between the Power and Petroleum Divisions.



\# Key Facts



\- The plan describes direct subsidy disbursement mechanisms for protected residential consumers.

\- The plan describes targeted subsidy disbursement for agricultural consumers.

\- The plan proposes progressively restricting cross-subsidies by productive consumers to 20% of the respective cost of service.

\- The extracted text does not clearly show the final year in Directive 84. The year requires verification against the original PDF.
