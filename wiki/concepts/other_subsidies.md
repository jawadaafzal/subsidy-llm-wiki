## Summary

Miscellaneous subsidies including Gilgit Baltistan wheat support, urea fertilizer imports, housing finance, export refinancing, and transport services for fiscal years 2025-26 and 2026-27.

## Main Content

- Total Others Subsidies are budgeted at Rs 205,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
- Wheat Subsidy to GB is budgeted at Rs 15,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
- Subsidy on Import of Urea Fertilizer is budgeted at Rs 10,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
- Mera Pakistan Mera Ghar Scheme (Mark-up Subsidy on Housing Finance Scheme) is budgeted at Rs 5,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
- Mark-up Subsidy and Risk Sharing Scheme for Farm Mechanization/Kissan Package is budgeted at Rs 5,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
- Refinance and Credit Guarantee Scheme (SME Asaan Finance) is budgeted at Rs 1,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
- Subsidy for enhancing financing to SME Sector is budgeted at Rs 2,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
- Markup Subsidy to Support Phasing out of SBP refinancing facilities/Export Re-finance Schemes (EFS) is budgeted at Rs 88,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
- Provision for 5km radius gas schemes is budgeted at Rs 1,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
- Metro Bus Subsidy is budgeted at Rs 5,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
- PM Apna Ghar Program is budgeted at Rs 71,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
- RSGT Claims is budgeted at Rs 1,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
- Other Subsidies is budgeted at Rs 1,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
