## Summary

Overview of budgetary allocations for power sector subsidies, tariff differentials, and related energy funds for fiscal years 2025-26 and 2026-27.

## Main Content

- The budget 2026-27 allocates Rs 830,000 million for the Subsidy to Power Sector (WAPDA/PEPCO/KESC).
- Subsidies for Tariff Differential to Agri Tube Wells in Balochistan (PEPCO) are budgeted at Rs 3,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
- Subsidies for Inter-DISCO Tariff Differential are budgeted at Rs 248,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
- Subsidies for Merged District of KP erstwhile FATA are budgeted at Rs 34,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
- Subsidies for Tariff Differential to AJK are budgeted at Rs 81,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
- Pakistan Energy Revolving Fund (PERA) is budgeted at Rs 48,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
- Subsidies to K-Electric for Tariff Differential are budgeted at Rs 163,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
- Subsidies to KESC for Tariff for Agriculture Tubes Wells in Balochistan are budgeted at Rs 1,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
- Allocation for containment of circular debt (CD) is budgeted at Rs 252,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
- Payment to IPPs is budgeted at Rs 0 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
