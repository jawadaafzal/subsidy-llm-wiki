## Summary

Overview of budgetary allocations for power sector subsidies, tariff differentials, cross-subsidies, and related energy funds for fiscal years 2025-26 and 2026-27, including implementation targets for consumer subsidy disbursement and subsidy rationalization policies under the National Electricity Plan 2023-27.

## Main Content

The budget 2026-27 allocates Rs 830,000 million for the Subsidy to Power Sector (WAPDA/PEPCO/KESC).
Subsidies for Tariff Differential to Agri Tube Wells in Balochistan (PEPCO) are budgeted at Rs 3,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
Subsidies for Inter-DISCO Tariff Differential are budgeted at Rs 248,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
Subsidies for Merged District of KP erstwhile FATA are budgeted at Rs 34,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
Subsidies for Tariff Differential to AJK are budgeted at Rs 81,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
Pakistan Energy Revolving Fund (PERA) is budgeted at Rs 48,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
Subsidies to K-Electric for Tariff Differential are budgeted at Rs 163,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
Subsidies to KESC for Tariff for Agriculture Tubes Wells in Balochistan are budgeted at Rs 1,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
Allocation for containment of circular debt (CD) is budgeted at Rs 252,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
Payment to IPPs is budgeted at Rs 0 million for fiscal year 2026-27.
Detailed action plan for subsidies disbursement for protected category of residential consumers is targeted for March 31st, 2024 under the National Electricity Plan 2023-27.
Subsidy qualification criteria and implementation plan for agricultural consumers is developed by May 31st, 2024 under the National Electricity Plan 2023-27.
Under the National Electricity Plan 2023-27, energy subsidies are identified as key policy instruments to meet government socio-economic objectives through alleviation of energy poverty and enabling access to affordable energy services.
Under the National Electricity Plan 2023-27, cross-subsidy by industrial and commercial consumers to subsidize residential and agricultural consumers is targeted to reach a limit of 20% by June 30th, 2026.
Under the National Electricity Plan 2023-27, captive units that receive government energy subsidies are prohibited from selling electric power in the power market.
The National Electricity Plan 2023-27 states that in a nation where millions depend on reliable electricity for their livelihoods, the NEPlan strategically emphasizes subsidy rationalization and financial viability.
The National Electricity Plan 2023-27 states that till the development of integrated IGCEP (including K-Electric), any decision for the retirement of generation projects from existing pool of XW-DISCOs for subsequent contract with K-Electric shall be made, taking into account the principle of least cost for consumers of both XW-DISCOs & K-Electric and consolidated subsidy requirements of Government.
The National Electricity Plan 2023-27 states that dedicated programs / instruments shall be developed by AEDB, in coordination with the Designated Entity and other relevant stakeholders, for provision of distributed generation for subsidized consumers / communities (lifeline / protected / agriculture).
The National Electricity Plan 2023-27 states that incentive mechanisms / schemes shall be utilized as a tool to facilitate accelerated energy transition and economic growth.
The plan states that tariffs for the residential consumers shall be progressively adjusted to align with the principle of cost-of-service under the National Electricity Plan 2023-27.
The plan states that subsidies to the agricultural consumers shall be disbursed pursuant to the detailed action plan to be developed under Strategic Directive 068 pursuant to the National Electricity Plan 2023-27.
The plan states that reappropriation of annual allocated subsidies between the Power and Petroleum Divisions of Ministry of Energy may be carried out to account for collective optimization of energy value chain under the National Electricity Plan 2023-27.
The plan states that in case the Government decides to reduce the open access charges or any of its components for the consumers opting for open access, it shall provide the funding to bridge the differential costs under the National Electricity Plan 2023-27.
The plan states that strategic directive 067 and its associated KPI provide for roadmap with specific timebound milestones to enable disbursement of subsidies for protected category of domestic consumers by FY-2026 under the National Electricity Plan 2023-27.
