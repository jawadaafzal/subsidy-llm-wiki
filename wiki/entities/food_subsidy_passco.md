## Summary

Budgetary allocations for PASSCO wheat reserves and cost differential sales for fiscal years 2025-26 and 2026-27.

## Main Content

PASSCO receives food subsidies for maintaining wheat reserve stocks and covering cost differentials for the sale of wheat. The total subsidy to food (PASSCO) is budgeted at Rs 19,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.

## Key Facts

- Total Subsidy to Food (PASSCO) is budgeted at Rs 19,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27. [pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27:615-615]
- Subsidy to PASSCO for Wheat Reserve Stock is budgeted at Rs 9,500 million for fiscal year 2026-27. [pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27:616-616]
- Subsidy to PASSCO on Account of Cost Differential for Sale of Wheat is budgeted at Rs 9,500 million for fiscal year 2026-27. [pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27:617-617]
