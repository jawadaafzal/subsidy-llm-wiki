## Summary

Allocations for fertilizer production, electric vehicle incentive schemes, and utility stores corporation (USC) arrears for fiscal years 2025-26 and 2026-27.

## Main Content

Subsidies to industries and production cover urea fertilizer production and supply, electric vehicle schemes, and USC arrears. The total subsidy for industries and production is budgeted at Rs 37,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27.

## Key Facts

- Total Subsidy to Industries & Production is budgeted at Rs 37,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27. [pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27:618-618]
- Production & Supply of Urea Fertilizer (Fertilizer Plants) subsidy is budgeted at Rs 5,800 million for fiscal year 2026-27. [pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27:619-619]
- Subsidy-Incentive on Electric Vehicle Scheme is budgeted at Rs 8,000 million for fiscal year 2026-27. [pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27:620-620]
- USC Arrears subsidy is budgeted at Rs 23,200 million for fiscal year 2026-27. [pakistan_federal_budget_2026_27:621-621]
