\# Planted Problems — Test Cases



These are intentionally incorrect or problematic statements.

They are NOT official government facts.



\## Problem 1: Incorrect Total

Claim: Pakistan's total budgeted subsidies for FY2026-27 are Rs 900,000 million.

Expected issue: Incorrect figure.

Verified figure: Rs 1,091,000 million.

Evidence: Federal Budget 2026-27, Table 12, pages 20-21.



\## Problem 2: Incorrect Power Subsidy

Claim: Power sector subsidies for FY2026-27 are Rs 700,000 million.

Expected issue: Incorrect figure.

Verified figure: Rs 830,000 million.

Evidence: Federal Budget 2026-27, Table 12.



\## Problem 3: Unsupported Payment Claim

Claim: The government has paid Rs 1,091,000 million directly to subsidy beneficiaries.

Expected issue: Budget allocation is being incorrectly presented as an actual payment.



\## Problem 4: Wrong Financial Year

Claim: The Rs 1,091,000 million subsidy allocation belongs to FY2025-26.

Expected issue: Incorrect financial year.

Verified year: FY2026-27.



\## Problem 5: Missing Citation

Claim: The government provides subsidies to support low-income households.

Expected issue: No supporting source or evidence is provided.
